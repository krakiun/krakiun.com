**Grain Exchange** is an app that completely changes how grain is traded in Romania.
It allows posting sale announcements from the planting phase – you don't have to wait for harvest. Essentially, farmers can sign **futures-type contracts**, and buyers can reserve clear quantities directly from the app.

---

## 📲 How does it work?

➡️ If you're a **farmer**, you list the grains you've planted (wheat, corn, rice, etc.) right now.
➡️ If you're a **buyer**, you can contract in advance what you need, at more stable prices, without uncertainty and without last-minute pressure.

Everything is designed for efficiency, clarity, and control. No intermediaries. No unnecessary auctions. No phone calls lost in the wind.

---

## 🤝 The advantages are clear:

- **Farmers** can secure their sales from the planting phase
- **Buyers** can reserve the desired goods in advance, negotiating directly and transparently
- **Both parties** avoid seasonal chaos, transport blockages, and unstable price pressure
