Time is the only asset we cannot recover. That's why I've built a rigorous filter to ensure that every conversation I have is one with impact.

This space is not for service offers, standard commercial proposals or superficial discussions. I don't sell time and I'm not looking for jobs. I build systems and long-term partnerships.

**I'm open to dialogue for three types of opportunities:**

1.  **Strategic Partnerships:** Collaboration proposals with vision, that can create exponentially greater value than the sum of its parts.
2.  **Investment Opportunities:** Discussions with professional investors who understand the tech startup ecosystem and can bring, besides capital, strategic value (smart money).
3.  **Disruptive Ideas:** Conversations with other entrepreneurs and thinkers who operate at the same level of complexity and have projects with potential to change an industry.

**How to contact me to receive a response:**

Your message must be clear, concise and demonstrate that you've understood what this is about. Present the opportunity directly, what's at stake and why you think a collaboration would make sense.

I respect your time as much as I respect mine. A well-structured message is the first proof of this mutual respect.
