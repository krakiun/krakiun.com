**Effective date: 19 September 2026**

## About the app

**Hotel Anvelope** (bundle id `com.hotel.anvelope`) is an app for tyre workshops that manages seasonal tyre storage (a "tyre hotel"). It is published by **Florentin Craciun** (krakiun.com), the product site is **anvelope.app**, and the app's servers run at **api.anvelope.app**.

## Who is responsible for the data

Data about a workshop's customers is entered by the workshop. For that data the **workshop is the data controller** and we act as the **processor** — we process it only to run the service, on the workshop's instructions. For account data (below) we are the controller.

## What we collect

**Account data:** e-mail address, name, password (stored hashed, never in clear text), chosen language, organisation/workshop name and the billing details entered by the user.

**Data entered by the workshop:** customers' names, phone numbers, e-mail addresses, vehicle licence plates, tyre set details, photos of tyre sets, on-screen signatures, recorded payments and notes. This data is stored on our servers (api.anvelope.app) so it syncs across the user's devices. Photos and signatures are served only through signed, expiring links, and only to signed-in members of the same workshop.

## Device permissions

The **camera** is used only to scan QR/barcode labels and to take photos that the user attaches to a tyre set. Nothing is recorded or uploaded without an explicit action by the user.

**Offline mode:** some data may be cached on the device so you can keep working without internet; it syncs automatically once you are back online.

## E-mails

We send transactional e-mail only: team invitations, password resets, and reminders the user chooses to send to their own customers. Delivery is handled by **Amazon SES**. We send no newsletters and no marketing.

## What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no sale of data, and no sharing with third parties beyond the hosting provider and the e-mail provider needed to run the service.

## Security

Traffic is encrypted with TLS. Passwords are stored hashed. Inside a workshop, access is role-based and significant actions are recorded in an audit log. Data is kept on secure servers operated by our hosting provider.

## Retention and deletion

Users can delete customers, tyre sets and photos at any time from within the app. A workshop owner can export all data (CSV/Excel/JSON) and can request full account deletion by writing to **contact@anvelope.app**. Deleted data is removed promptly from active systems and from backups within a reasonable period, normally no more than 30 days.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, port and object to the processing of your personal data. If you are a customer of a workshop that uses the app, please contact that workshop first; we support them in answering you. For any request, write to **contact@anvelope.app**.

## Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at anyone under 16.
