For years I've been working in online, in technology, in digital strategies.

I've learned to see what others ignore: problems, opportunities, gaps, and solutions that can be turned into business.

**What I cannot do – and don't want to do – is implement everything by myself.**

---

## What I'm Looking For:

I'm looking for an **execution partner** — a serious, hardworking, organized person who takes a ready-made plan and turns it into a functional business.

I'm not looking for dreamers.
I'm not looking for idealists.
I'm looking for **someone who executes**. Who knows how to get things done. Who loves work and doesn't run from effort. A **fighter**.

---

## What I Bring to the Table:

- A **Validated / Tested Business Idea** that can produce millions of euros.
- A **functional product** – MVP ready to launch
- A **tested business plan** – clear, validated direction
- A **prepared marketing plan**, immediately applicable
- **Ideally you have a team**. - Optional, but a big plus.
- Strategic support
- Direction. Clarity. Know-how.

You don't have to come with the idea, nor with the money.
All you need to bring is **real involvement and execution capacity**.

---

## What You Need to Know and Be Able to Do:

- Have **good communication skills** – including phone calls with potential partners, clients, or collaborators
- Be **financially independent for a few months** – meaning not be pressured by bills
- You don't have to invest money in the business. But you have to invest time, energy, and discipline
- Have **real skills in IT and online marketing** – not diplomas, but the ability to figure things out on your own
- Know how to run campaigns, search, implement, find solutions
- Have experience in **sales, marketing, execution**
- Not need someone explaining every day what you have to do

I'm not looking for "superheroes."
But neither people who need to be pushed from behind.
I'm looking for someone who can function independently but builds as a team.

---

## How the Partnership Will Work:

- It can go up to **35% of the business**, offered **in stages** based on real results
- You don't receive anything just for "being here"
- Everything is granted on **clear milestones**: MVP launched, active customers, generated revenues, etc.
- If in **3 months** you haven't launched anything, the collaboration stops
- If in **12 months** there isn't an active business, everything is canceled
- A **non-disclosure agreement (NDA)** and a **partnership agreement** with clear terms will be signed
