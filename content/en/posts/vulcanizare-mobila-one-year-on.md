Almost a year after launching [vulcanizaremobila.com](https://vulcanizaremobila.com), it's time for an honest assessment. Not a press release, not a polished success story — but a real look at where we are, what worked, and what we're building next.

## What Is Vulcanizare Mobilă

A digital platform that connects drivers with flat tires to verified mobile tire repair professionals available in their area — in real time. The idea is simple. The execution, less so.

The mobile tire repair market in Romania was — and largely still is — **fragmented, informal, and hard to access** when you actually need it. We built the missing infrastructure: real-time service requests, geographic zone allocation, live partner availability monitoring, a review system, and a complete admin panel.

## The Numbers After Year One

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Interventions processed | **2,000+** |
| Partners in the network | **48 mobile tire technicians** |
| Available in real time | **43 out of 48** |
| Coverage zones | **76 active zones** |
| Geographic coverage | **All of Romania** |

An **89% real-time availability rate** (43 out of 48 partners active simultaneously) is an operational metric we track closely — it reflects the reliability of the network, not just its size.

The partner map covers every region of the country: from Maramureș and Satu Mare in the north to Constanța and Tulcea in the southeast, with high density in metropolitan areas and presence in mid-sized cities.

## Organic Growth — What the Data Shows

Without aggressive paid advertising, the platform has grown steadily in organic visibility over the past 12 months:

- **622,000 impressions** in Google Search (June 2025 – June 2026)
- **10,900 organic clicks**
- **Average position: 8.7** — continuously improving
- **Clear upward trend** — volume in the last 3 months exceeds the first 6 months combined

This means search engines are validating the platform as a relevant resource in the niche. The growth isn't artificial — it's built on real content, real reviews, and real platform activity.

## What We're Not Publishing and Why

We're not publishing revenue figures. The reasons are straightforward:

**We don't have spectacular revenue to report.** For a significant period we operated with free services for clients — a deliberate decision to build real volume, authentic reviews, and market credibility before monetizing.

**We're in the growth phase, not profit optimization.** Year one's priority was nationwide coverage and network quality. We achieved both.

We think it's more honest to say this plainly than to present small numbers as major achievements.

## Where We're Going: B2B and Scale

The growth vector we're pursuing now is the **business segment**: companies with vehicle fleets, leasing operators, insurers, transport and logistics firms. Their need for fast, predictable tire interventions is real and recurring — exactly the type of demand our platform is built for.

We're in active discussions with partners in this segment. We can't share details at this stage, but the direction is clear.

## Lessons from Year One

**What worked:**
- Geographic zone architecture — scalable and easy to expand to new cities
- Structured partner onboarding — reduces drop-off and raises network quality
- The decision to grow organically before monetizing aggressively

**What we underestimated:**
- The time needed to educate the market — both clients and mobile tire technicians are unfamiliar with a digital platform in this space
- Informal resistance from some market players toward digitalization and transparency
- The operational complexity of maintaining availability in less densely covered areas

## Conclusion

Vulcanizare Mobilă has moved past the validation phase. There's a functioning national network, real demand, and a stable platform processing interventions every day.

**Year two** means consolidating the network, closing the first B2B contracts, and preparing the infrastructure for scale. We're not claiming to have transformed the market — but we've built the right foundation to do it.
